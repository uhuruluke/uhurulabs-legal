Privacy Policy — Scope Creep Detector for Jira

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how UhuruLabs (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you install or use Scope Creep Detector for Jira (“the App”).

1. What the App does

The App compares a Jira issue’s current description with a saved baseline to identify possible scope expansion and display the analysis inside Jira.

2. Information the App accesses and stores

The App accesses Jira issue content needed for scope analysis, including the issue description. To provide baseline comparison, the App stores a copy of the selected baseline description together with limited analysis metadata associated with the Jira issue and App installation.

Jira issue descriptions may contain information entered by customers or end users, including personal information if users choose to place such information in an issue. The App does not require users to enter personal information for its own purposes.

3. Where data is processed

The App is built on Atlassian Forge. App logic and persistent app storage use Atlassian-hosted Forge services. Scope Creep Detector does not send Jira issue content to an external AI service, external analytics provider, advertising network, or a separate UhuruLabs application server.

4. How information is used

Information accessed by the App is used only to provide scope-comparison functionality, maintain the saved baseline selected by the user, display analysis results, operate and secure the App, and respond to support or privacy requests.

5. Sharing and sale of data

UhuruLabs does not sell customer or end-user data. The App does not use Jira content for advertising and does not disclose Jira issue content to third-party advertising or data-broker services.

6. Retention and deletion

Baseline data is retained only as needed to provide the App’s functionality. Users can replace a stored baseline through the App. Data associated with an installation is also subject to Atlassian Forge storage and app lifecycle controls. Customers may contact us to request assistance with deletion of App-stored data where applicable.

7. Security

The App uses Atlassian Forge-hosted execution and storage and requests only the Jira and Forge permissions required for its functionality. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we aim to minimize the amount of data processed and avoid unnecessary external data transfers.

8. International use

Atlassian may process Forge-hosted data in accordance with Atlassian’s own infrastructure, security, data residency, and privacy practices. Customers should review Atlassian’s applicable documentation for details relevant to their organization.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to processing of personal data. Requests concerning data stored specifically by Scope Creep Detector can be sent to the contact below. Requests concerning data controlled by an Atlassian customer should normally be directed to that customer first.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves or legal requirements change. The current version will remain available at this URL with an updated effective date.
